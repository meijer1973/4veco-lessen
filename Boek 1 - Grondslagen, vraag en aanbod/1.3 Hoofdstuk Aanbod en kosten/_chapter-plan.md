## Shared conventions
- Notation: Qa = aangeboden hoeveelheid, Qv = gevraagde hoeveelheid, P = prijs.
- Cost notation: CK, VK, TCK, TVK, TK, GCK, GVK, GTK.
- Revenue notation: TO, GO, winst = TO - TK.
- Function form: write supply as Qa = aP - b or Qa = a + bP when algebra is used.
- Axis convention: price on the vertical axis, quantity on the horizontal axis.
- Supply colour: #27AE60.
- Demand colour when reused: #1A5276.
- Costs/TK: #E67E22. Revenue/TO: #1A5276. Profit shading: #27AE60 at low opacity; loss shading: #E74C3C at low opacity.

## Procedure plan
- Aanbodverandering classificeren:
  1. Is de eigen prijs veranderd?
  2. Ja: beweging langs de aanbodlijn. Nee: kijk welke aanbodfactor verandert.
  3. Bepaal richting: aanbod stijgt naar rechts, aanbod daalt naar links.
- Kosten berekenen:
  1. Bereken TVK = VK per stuk x Q.
  2. Bereken TK = TCK + TVK.
  3. Bereken GCK = TCK / Q, GVK = TVK / Q, GTK = TK / Q.
- Opbrengst en winst berekenen:
  1. Bereken TO = P x Q.
  2. Bereken TK met de kostenprocedure.
  3. Bereken winst = TO - TK.
- Break-even berekenen:
  1. Stel TO gelijk aan TK.
  2. Vul P x Q en TCK + GVK x Q in.
  3. Los op naar Q en controleer met TO en TK.

## Precision standards
- Keep total, average, and marginal language separated; do not say "costs rise" when the intended claim is about average costs.
- State units on axes and in calculations.
- For supply curves, keep movement along the curve separate from curve shifts.
- For break-even, state the constant-price and constant-variable-cost assumptions when using the simple formula.
- Make clear that spreading fixed costs lowers GCK as Q rises, while total fixed costs remain constant.
