## Pass 2: Mathematical and Conceptual Precision
- PASS - Vraag (a) calculations checked: TK at Q = 200 is 1400 and GTK = 7; TK at Q = 400 is 2000 and GTK = 5.
- PASS - Vraag (b) equilibrium checked: `-40P + 580 = 20P - 20` gives P = 10 and Q = 180.
- PASS - Vraag (c) new equilibrium checked: `-40P + 580 = 20P - 60` gives P = 32/3 = 10.67 and Q = 460/3 = 153.33.
- PASS - The graph coordinates in fig_1 and ex_1 match the stated functions and equilibrium points.
- PASS - Vraag (e) percentage change is correct after rounding: (10.67 - 10) / 10 = about 6.7%.
- FAIL - Vraag (d) conflates firm-level and market-level reasoning. The context says a small coffee roaster operates in volkomen concurrentie, but the question asks whether this firm should produce more or less than the market equilibrium quantity of 153 bags. The answer model then says the firm would like to produce as much as the market demands. A price-taking firm does not individually choose the whole market quantity; with TK = 800 + 3Q and P = 10.67, MO > MK for every unit, so there is no finite optimum without a capacity constraint, individual demand/sales limit, or firm-specific output target. Fix by either making the coffee roaster the whole market/only supplier, adding a capacity or market-share constraint, or changing the question to "is an extra bag profitable at this price?"

## Summary
The integration exercise has a strong single context and mostly correct calculations, but it is blocked by two issues: missing required chapter-skill labels and a conceptual error in the marginal-analysis question that mixes market quantity with an individual firm's production decision. Those should be fixed before student use.
