## Pass 1: Didactic Architecture
- PASS - toetsmatrijs.md is present and contains a Leerdoel x Bloom x Punten matrix.
- PASS - All four theory chapters are represented in the matrix: 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, and 1.4.
- PASS - Source types are varied: graph context, cost/MK tables, news text, index series, and betalingsbereidheid table.

- FLAG - Bloom distribution is skewed toward toepassen/analyseren. The matrix reports Begrijpen 11%, Toepassen 49%, Analyseren 40%, compared with the target of about 25% / 40% / 35%.
- FLAG - Total size is large for a 120-minute formative practice test at VWO 4 start level: 74 points and 25 subquestions. This may be defensible as a full mock exam, but it is heavier than the chapter plan target of about 40 points.

## Pass 2: Mathematical and Conceptual Precision
- PASS - Opgave 1 calculations checked: GTK, break-even Q = 300, profit at P = 15 and P = 12, and GCK/GVK decomposition are correct.
- PASS - Opgave 2 equilibrium and shift calculations checked: original P = 40/Q = 40; demand shift gives P = 46.67/Q = 53.33; supply shift gives P = 46.67/Q = 33.33; minimum price P = 60 gives an aanbodoverschot of 60.
- PASS - Opgave 4 percentage/index calculations checked: 1.80 to 2.02 is 12.2%, approximately 12%; 120 vs 108 gives 11.1%, not 12%.
- PASS - Opgave 5 consumer-surplus calculations checked: CS is 110 euro at P = 80 and 210 euro at P = 50.
- FAIL - Opgave 3 has inconsistent marginal-cost units. The test states that MK and MO are calculated "per blok van 5 stuks" and the table header says "MK (per 5 stuks, euro)", but the values 5, 15, 25, 35 are marginal costs per single unit averaged over each 5-unit interval. If the column were truly per 5 units, the marginal costs would be 25, 75, 125, and 175, and MO per 5 units would be 150. The answer model later says "per stuk", which confirms the intended interpretation. Fix the table header and prompt to "MK per stuk over het laatste blok van 5 stuks" or adjust all values to block totals.
- FLAG - Vraag 17 uses `MK approx 2Q` after presenting a discrete MK table in 5-unit blocks. The approximation is acceptable for a first algebraic rule, but the table/unit inconsistency above must be resolved so students know when they are using discrete differences versus a marginal formula.

## Summary
The proeftoets has good coverage and mostly reliable calculations, but it is blocked by a technical unit inconsistency in the marginal-cost table. It also deviates materially from the target practice-test shape: more contexts, many more subquestions, fewer 2-point items than intended, and a heavier Bloom profile.
