## Consolidated Verdict
- Verdict: **REVISE**
- Reason: The web presentation is platform-generated, source-reproducible, linked correctly, and passes current automated tests, but the mobile render clips essential slide content and navigation controls. This violates the L1.5D requirement that authored content work on mobile and desktop and is a student-facing usability blocker.

## Blocking Findings
- **PW-B1. Mobile rendering clips essential slide 5 content and controls.**
  - Evidence: Chrome headless mobile screenshot `C:\tmp\Codex-work\review-111-presentatie-web-l15d-20260511\screens\presentatie-mobile-slide-5.png` at 390px width.
  - Observed result: the slide 5 title `Economisch denken in vier stappen` is cut off on the right, the `Volgende` button is cut off on the right, and the slide visual extends/crops horizontally instead of fitting the viewport.
  - Related source: `presentatie.html:149-182` contains the slide 5 card stack and figure; presentation layout CSS starts at `engines\voorkennis.css:1529`, with the slide container and controls at `:1532-1586`.
  - Impact: students on a phone cannot reliably read or navigate the web presentation. This is a direct failure of the web surface acceptance requirement for mobile and desktop.

## Non-Blocking Flags
- **PW-F1. Text-to-speech is not present.**
  - Evidence: HTML parse found `has_speech False`.
  - Impact: not blocking because the roadmap describes Web Speech as a stretch/progressive enhancement, not core acceptance.
- **PW-F2. Current automated tests do not catch mobile overflow.**
  - Evidence: `presentatie-html-shape.test.js` validates slide count, notes, alt text, dark/light counts, card stack, and pseudotable structure, but the observed clipping was only found in rendered mobile QA.
  - Impact: add a mobile screenshot or DOM overflow check before closing v2 so this regression does not return quietly.

## Specialist Findings
- **Presentation structure:** Current HTML uses `data-layout="presentatie-v1"` (`presentatie.html:15`), has slide controls at `:48-50` and `:322-324`, 9 slide sections, 9 structured note blocks, 5 figures, and a PowerPoint download link.
- **Visual QA:** Desktop screenshots for slides 5, 6, and 8 are readable and visually coherent. Mobile slide 5 fails due clipping.
- **Accessibility:** Image alt text is meaningful, including the slide 5 four-step flowchart alt at `presentatie.html:182` and the worked-example visual at `:209`. Speaker notes are exposed as structured text. Controls have usable labels. Mobile clipping remains an accessibility/usability blocker.

- **Routing:** Paragraph `index.html` routes the primary presentation card to `presentatie.html` and keeps the `.pptx` as a download. Local href checks found no missing presentation targets.
