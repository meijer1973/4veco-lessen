from phase and slide number; current-phase emphasis is intentional. The start
retrieves individual supply, substitution and technique shifts taught on
87–88. It does not assume that the existence of a prior exercise proves mastery.

Slide **2** recalls source/method selection and the known two-point graph method.
Slides **3–5** use an explicitly labelled authored example, **vouwkrukjes**:
Qv = 72 − 4P, Qa,0 = 8P − 24, Qa,1 = 8P − 12. Equilibria are (40; 8) and
(44; 7); at the old price the new supply is 52. This is separate context/data,
not an answer to an assigned textbook exercise. Notes identify its provenance.
The recall stays within earlier teaching; it introduces no cost/surplus theory.

scenario resets, conditional trade rule, two-part quotation and irrelevant
survey datum. Slides **11–13** contain all six actual questions, before the
first answer on slide **14**.
