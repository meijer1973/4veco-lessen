for 0 ≤ P ≤ 5 and qB = 14 − 2P for 0 ≤ P ≤ 7. Quantities are liters per week,

other circumstances stay constant. Slides label it `Uitlegvoorbeeld — niet uit
het boek`, and notes distinguish authored data from book methods. It does not
work out an assigned practice question.
