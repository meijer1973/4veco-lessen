## Shared context and model conditions

The concert demand P=50−0.5Q is reused deliberately across231–233.
P is €/kaartje; Q is kaartjes; CS/PS/TS/DWL are euros, not €/kaartje.

- 231: given P20, enough tickets, all60 demanded tickets sold to the highest-WTP
  buyers. No supply function: Q60 is quantity demanded, not calculated equilibrium.
  Formal CS is the area under demand and above price over realized transactions.
  Teach discrete individual benefits before the continuous linear-area model;
  distinguish an area approximation/model from simply counting buyers.
- 232: add P=5+0.25Q supply. State that this inverse supply represents the MC of
  each successive unit over0≤Q≤100, including untraded units. Qe60/Pe20,
  CS900/PS450/TS1,350. AtQ50 WTP25 > MC17.50; atQ70 WTP15 < MC22.50.
  TS maximum is a claim within this model, not universal welfare or fairness.
  PS is not automatically profit; costs covered by supply/MC and fixed costs
  are different concepts. Do not introduce firm-level output optimization.
- 233: fixed price25 plus booking limit40; technical capacity at least60;
  costless removable restriction; highest-WTP buyers and lowest-MC suppliers;
  existing transactions and price stay unchanged upon relaxation. Qd50 and
  Qs80 do not make tradedQ50: the stricter booking rule fixes40.
  WTP(Q40)=30 andMC(Q40)=15. CS600, PS600, TS1,200, DWL150.
  A41st trade at25 gives WTP29.50 andMC15.25; both new parties gain and
  existing parties are not harmed under the explicit feasibility assumptions.
- 234: new rental-bike context P=80−Q and P=20+0.5Q; freeQ40/Pe40,
  CS800/PS400/TS1,200. Bookinglimit30 atP45, capacity≥40, same allocation
  and costless-expansion safeguards. RestrictedCS600/PS525/TS1,125/DWL75.
  Given31st-unit WTP49 andMC35.50 support a mutually beneficial trade at45.
  HigherPS alone cannot establish efficiency; blue bicycles are irrelevant.

Non-goals: fairness proof, interpersonal utility comparison, externalities,
tax/subsidy/price-control mechanics, universal any-price-causes-DWL claims,
calculus, profit maximization, OP-C2. A price change alone is not a complete
transaction/allocation model. A restriction must be binding before reducing
trades; a formally stated constraint is not automatically an immutable limit
on feasible improvements. Keep economic feasibility and the removable rule clear.

## Unified procedures and interleaving

Quantity at price: identify the demand/supply relation → substitute givenP →
solveQ → verify → name quantity demanded/offered, not equilibrium unless both
relations were equated. Equilibrium: set inverse demand=supply → solveQ →
substitute forP → check both relations → contextual quantity/price units.

Surplus: establish actual trades AND allocation → identify beneficiary area →
label base/height with units → use triangle or rectangle+triangle → sum euros →
interpret WTP−price or price−MC over those trades. TS=CS+PS. DWL=referenceTS−
actualTS, independently checked by the omitted-trade triangle when applicable.
Pareto application: identify feasible new parties/transaction → compare WTP,
price andMC → show both benefits → verify no harm to existing parties and no
expansion cost → distinguish efficiency from a fairness norm.

Repeat these procedures across theory/example/answers; fade assistance rather
than changing steps. Closing retrieval in231 revisits equation/axis units;
232 deliberately reconnects marginal meaning from213;233 compares quantity
demanded, supplied and actually traded;234 interleaves source selection and
qualified claims across the chapter. No unrelated arithmetic filler.
