### Model, sources and stable procedures

Every market below is a fictional one-day model of identical units: inverse
demand is WTP of successive units and inverse supply is each successive unit's
MC over the entire stated range, including untraded units. Other conditions
are held fixed; no external effects or transaction costs. Highest-WTP buyers
and lowest-MC suppliers receive/provide actual units; uniform price is stated.
The administrative booking cap is costlessly removable and distinct from
technical capacity, which is at least the free quantity unless explicitly
changed in guided5. On the tested extra trade, price and all existing trades
and their participants' benefits remain unchanged; nobody bears hidden costs.
State these conditions beside first functions and in each exercise source.

Q means units during that day, not thousands; P/WTP/MC are €/unit; CS/PS/TS/DWL
are euros. Continuous area is a stated smooth model, not the exact discrete
sum of ordinal unit-point values. In target e use the supplied functions at41
exactly:29.50 and15.25. Do not replace41 by40 or equate this point gap14.25 with
the finite continuous integral over40..41 (14.625). No calculus is pupil work.

Four stable procedure families, same labels/order in theory/example/answers:

1. **Bepaal wat werkelijk gebeurt.** Select givenP/functions for Qd/Qs; solve
   and check quantities; apply booking AND capacity AND the stated allocation.
   Name planned demand/offered quantity separately from actual sales.
2. **Bereken voordeel van de handel.** Read WTP andMC at actualq. CS lies
   between demand and price; PS between price and supply, only0..q. In these
   binding examples WTP(q)>P>MC(q): CS=q×(WTP(q)−P)+½q×(WTP(0)−WTP(q));
   PS=q×(P−MC(q))+½q×(MC(q)−MC(0)). State this domain/shape condition WITH the
   formulas; not a universal trapezoid shortcut for any allocation or price.
3. **Vergelijk de toestanden.** TS=CS+PS; DWL=free benchmarkTS−actualTS. For
   these linear efficient allocations at q<Qe, check missing triangle between
   demand/supply overq..Qe: baseQe−q, heightWTP(q)−MC(q). Shade THAT region,
   not an axis-to-q surplus, price-transfer rectangle or Qd-to-Qs gap.
4. **Toets de verbetering en begrens je conclusie.** Define Pareto efficiency;
   for the named extra trade compute both new parties' benefits, then explicitly
   verify feasible capacity, costless rule change and unchanged existing parties.
   At least one strict gain and no loss is required; both gain in these examples.
   A higher aggregate TS alone does not establish these person-level conditions;
   neither efficiency nor TS specifies a fairness norm.

The free equilibrium is a supplied comparison state, not a redundant new solve
task. At the higher FIXED price, removing a booking cap does NOT necessarily
restore freeQe: e.g. targetP25 supports Qd50, not60. The41st trade suffices to
disprove efficiency of40; do not claim all40..60 can trade at25 with both gains.
Changing the price to20 would change existing parties and cannot be smuggled
into that proof. Money redistribution alone, with the same goods/costs, makes
one party gain at another's expense; do not equate it with a Pareto improvement.

Non-goals: profit optimization/MO=MK/OPC2, policy/tax/subsidy mechanics, universal
price-causes-DWL claims, fairness proof or interpersonal utility comparison,
unmodelled externalities, calculus. PS is not profit: absent fixed costs are
unknown, not zero. Higher PS alone is neither greaterTS nor Pareto efficiency.
