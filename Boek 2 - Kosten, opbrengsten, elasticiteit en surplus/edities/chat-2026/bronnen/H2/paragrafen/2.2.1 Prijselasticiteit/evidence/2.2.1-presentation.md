## Teaching and target coverage

The 22-slide sequence starts with the common overview (slide 1), develops the
goals and a separate teaching example (2–11), repeats the overview for work
(12), shows all actual target data and questions (13–15), gives the stepped
discussion (16–21), and closes with the same overview (22).

KleiKamer and FotoFlex are authored examples, labelled `Uitlegvoorbeeld — niet
uit het boek` and identified in notes/manifest. No assigned exercise 1–7 is
worked as the explanatory example. KleiKamer uses €40 to €44 per visit and 300
to 282 visits per month: +10%, −6%, Ev = −0.6. FotoFlex supplies a separate
fictitious Ev = −1.4. A separate price-decrease check uses €40 to €36 and 300
to 345 visits: −10%, +15%, Ev = −1.5. It is explicitly a separate scenario.

Theory includes old-value denominators, the sign and unit-free interpretation
of Ev, direct comparisons with −1 and 0, unit elasticity and zero response,
ceteris paribus, relative versus absolute comparisons, and possible versus
proven causes. No demand graph is required by the target operation; editable
comparison tables and calculation sequences supply the representations.

Target questions a–d match the manuscript, including point values. Slide 14
retains Ev = −0.8 because it is already supplied by the actual question b.
There is no worked answer before slide 16.

| Target | Discussion and check |
|---|---|
| 7a | `(12−10)/10×100% = +20%`; `(420−500)/500×100% = −16%`; `Ev = −16%/+20% = −0.8`. Units and week period stated, reverse checks included. |
| 7b | `−1 < −0.8 < 0`, price-inelastic; the percentage quantity fall is 0.8 times the price rise in this measurement. No guaranteed future response. |
| 7c | StreamNow `−2 < −1`, price-elastic and relatively more sensitive than Nova; no absolute lost-customer inference. |
| 7d | Plausible substitution/cancellation explanation, explicitly conditional; no invented proven motive or other elasticity calculation. |
