## 4. Concepts and terminology

| Concept | Canonical meaning | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
|Table interval|Two stated quantities for comparable same-day alternatives|Not successive days, a point derivative or one individual unit|
|Delta|End minus start in matching rows|Use the same positive deltaQ in each ratio|
|MK|DeltaTK/deltaQ in euros per extra product within that interval|Not TK/Q or raw deltaTK|
|MO|DeltaTO/deltaQ in euros per extra product within that interval|Constant only under the stated fixed-price/sold-quantity model|
|Profit|TO-TK at the same Q, in euros for the whole comparison|Signed total, not per-product marginal growth|
|GTK|TK/Q for all positive-Q products|Distinct from incremental MK; undefined at Q0|
|Profit-growth bridge|Delta profit/deltaQ=MO-MK over the same interval|Not total profit, an individual increment or an optimization theorem|
|Right endpoint|Display convention for the preceding interval|Does not identify that endpoint product's own cost|
