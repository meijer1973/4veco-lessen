## 4. Concepts and terminology

| Concept | Accepted meaning and formula | Boundary / avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Totale opbrengst | TO = P × Q for one price and matching period | Not profit; not a price on the total-euro graph axis |
| Gemiddelde opbrengst | GO = TO/Q = P for Q > 0 and fixed P | Not defined by division at Q = 0 |
| Totale winst / verlies | TW = TO − TK, signed total for the same period | Negative result is loss, not negative revenue |
| Break-even | TO = TK, so TW = 0 | Revenue is generally positive; equality is not positive profit |
| Winstafstand | Vertical TO − TK at one chosen Q | Not the whole area between curves or a length detached from scale |
| Hele aantallen | Check adjacent feasible integers around exact crossing | Do not blindly round down or round intermediate calculations |

Left/right profit language applies only within the given model/range and
its increasing-profit condition. Broader nonlinear functions can behave
differently. Neutral Dutch help wording treats mistakes as repairable practice.
