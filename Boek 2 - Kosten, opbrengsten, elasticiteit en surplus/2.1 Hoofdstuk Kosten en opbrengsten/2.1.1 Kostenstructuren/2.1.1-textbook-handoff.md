## 4. Concepts and terminology

| Concept | Student-facing meaning | Formula | Canonical term / avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Constant cost | Total amount does not change with Q within stated period/capacity | TCK | constante kosten; not simply 'a bill paid monthly' |
| Variable cost | Total amount changes with Q under the chosen contract | TVK = vQ in this model | variabele kosten; not every changed contract price |
| Total cost | Constant plus variable total for the same period | TK = TCK + TVK | totale kosten; not an average |
| Average constant cost | Matching fixed total per product | GCK = TCK/Q | gemiddelde constante kosten; not another invoice |
| Average variable cost | Matching variable total per product | GVK = TVK/Q | gemiddelde variabele kosten; not universally constant |
| Average total cost | Matching total cost per product | GTK = TK/Q = GCK + GVK | gemiddelde totale kosten; does not generally halve |

Totals use euro per month here; averages use euro per named product. Q is
positive when dividing. 'Constant' classifies behavior against Q, not across
different contracts. A fixed variable cost per product is an explicit model
assumption, never an unrestricted economic law.
