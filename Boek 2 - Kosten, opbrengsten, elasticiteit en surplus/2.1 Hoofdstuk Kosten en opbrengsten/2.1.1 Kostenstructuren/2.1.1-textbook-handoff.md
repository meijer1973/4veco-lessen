## 4. Concepts And Terminology

| Concept | Definition / formula | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| TCK | Total constant cost in a period; unchanged with Q within capacity | Treating a fixed total as a fixed amount per product |
| TVK | Total variable cost; here vQ because v is assumed constant | Calling all energy constant or variable by label alone |
| TK | TCK + TVK | Confusing total and average |
| GCK / GVK / GTK | Corresponding total divided by Q, for Q>0 | Omitting euro per product or assuming all GVK constant |
