### Exercise, explanation, and worked-example sequence

#### Explanation design and whole-lesson budget

Motivation (2 min): a poster atelier asks whether twice as many posters means
twice as high a total bill and half the cost per poster. Do not answer by slogan.
Name the month, Q = 40–80 posters/month and unchanged capacity. Use fixed costs
€ 120/month and variable materials € 2/poster consistently throughout theory.

Formal instruction (12 min, four 3-minute blocks):

1. Define constant/variable costs by response of the total to Q in this month/range; separate an energy subscription from usage. The invoice label is not the criterion.
2. Build TCK = 120, TVK = 2Q, TK = 120 + 2Q step by step. At Q = 40 totals are 120, 80, 200 euro/month; at 80 they are 120, 160, 280. Table and staged total-cost figure encode those same numbers.
3. Define GCK = TCK/Q, GVK = TVK/Q, GTK = TK/Q = GCK + GVK. Show the dimension (euro/month)/(posters/month) = euro/poster. At 40 averages are 3, 2, 5; at 80 they are 1,50, 2, 3,50 euro/poster.
4. Explain each change causally and bind it to this model. Display average-cost comparison after establishing division; confront “all averages halve” and “GVK is always constant.” Do not introduce a formal curve taxonomy.

Each definition gets one compact definition box; two formula boxes contain
at most three relations each; validity conditions accompany the first formula.
Warnings immediately follow the misconception they address. The economics
precision reference §§7.3–7.5 overrides a generic graph recipe demanding a
U-shaped GTK: in this bounded linear model GTK decreases and GVK is constant.

Uitgewerkt voorbeeld (8 min): a bicycle repair atelier performs Q = 100–200
repairs in one month at unchanged capacity. Workspace rent € 180/month,
insurance € 20/month, cleaning materials € 1/repair and small parts € 1/repair.
Name Q as repairs/month. Explicitly demonstrate all five target stages:
classification/reasons; TCK = 200, TVK = 2Q, TK = 200 + 2Q; total rows
(200,200,400) and (200,400,600); average rows (2,2,4) and (1,2,3)
euro/repair; then constant/doubled/rising totals and three bounded average
explanations. Use a thought-aloud choice of numerator and unit check, not
unexplained substitution. No new operation, graph construction or profit.

Compact non-heading summary/transitions (2 min): maximum five points covering
behavior/conditions; total functions; average formulas/units; conditional
doubling comparison; forward pointer to §2.1.2 without teaching revenue now.
Place it directly after the worked example and before Startopgaven.

2 motivation + 12 instruction + 8 example + 2 summary/transitions
