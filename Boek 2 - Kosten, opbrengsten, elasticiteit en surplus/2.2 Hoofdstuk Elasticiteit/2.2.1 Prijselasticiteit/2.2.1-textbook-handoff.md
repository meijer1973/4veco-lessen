## 4. Concepts and terminology

| Concept | Accepted meaning | Boundary / avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Procentuele verandering | (new − old)/old × 100%, using each variable's own old base | Positive old P and Q; not percentage points |
| Prijselasticiteit van de vraag | Ev = %ΔQv / %ΔP | Nonzero price change; signed and dimensionless |
| Teken | Direction of quantity response relative to own-price change | Do not drop minus before reporting Ev |
| Prijsinelastisch | |Ev| < 1, relatively smaller quantity response | Compare magnitudes, not signed numerical order |
| Prijselastisch | |Ev| > 1, relatively larger quantity response | Not a claim based on graph steepness alone |
| Eenheidselasticiteit | |Ev| = 1 | Boundary kept distinct |
| Mogelijke verklaring | Contextual substitute/necessity reasoning within supplied evidence | Plausible mechanism is not causal proof |

Own price is not another good's price. Signed Ev and magnitude-based price
classification here must not be exported as an absolute-value rule for Ei:
§2.2.3 classifies income elasticity using its sign and stated boundaries.
