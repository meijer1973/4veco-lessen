# Paragraph Plan: 2.2.3 Inkomenselasticiteit en kruiselingse elasticiteit

## Scope

This paragraph teaches income elasticity, cross-price elasticity, and a bounded demand-function route. It builds on 2.2.1 signed elasticity and 2.2.2 answer structure, but it does not introduce omzet advice beyond brief contrast.

## Student Route

1. Recall the shared elasticity structure.
2. Teach `Ei = %dQv / %dY`.
3. Classify `Ei` by sign first: inferior if negative; necessity/luxury only for positive normal goods.
4. Teach `Ek = %dQv of product A / %dP of product B`.
5. Classify `Ek` as substitutes or complements.
6. Scaffold demand-function use through a one-variable-at-a-time ceteris paribus route.
7. Practice with start, independent, target, and stretch exercises.

## Target Trace

The doeloefening mirrors the migrated 2.2.3 target while repairing the ambiguous inferior-good wording:

- organic meal packs: positive `Ei` and luxury normal good;
- budget noodles: negative `Ei`, therefore inferior good and not luxury/necessity;
- tea/coffee and filters/coffee cross elasticity;
- demand function `Qx = 100 - 2Px + 0,5Py + 0,01Y`;
- income and cross-price changes handled separately with other variables constant.
