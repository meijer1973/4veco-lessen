#### Hook and staged theory (2 + 11 minutes)

Hook: “De prijzen zijn gelijk gebleven, maar mensen vragen toch andere aantallen.
Komt dat door hun inkomen, of door de prijs van een ander goed?” Two clearly
separate fictional changes: income rises and demand for a chosen purchase changes;
an alternative becomes dearer and demand for this good changes. Ask which
percentage belongs below the fraction. Do not demand an untaught ratio answer.
Print the four goals within the first half page.

Before dependent use print a compact recall box: §221 used quantity change over
own-price change, with signed `(nieuw−oud)/oud×100%`; Book1 distinguishes
substitutes (replace each other) and complements (used together). For substitution,
replace every occurrence, multiply each coefficient first, then add/subtract
the terms and include the output unit. Demonstrate Q=60−2P at P5:60−10=50
stuks/week. This is a printed reminder, not a demand to remember another file.

#### Uitgewerkt voorbeeld: W1–W3 (8 minutes total)

W1 (1.5min): income+10%; artisanal chocolate Q+15%, instant soup Q−5%, other
