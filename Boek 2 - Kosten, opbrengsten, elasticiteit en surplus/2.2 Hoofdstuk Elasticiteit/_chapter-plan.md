# Chapter Plan: 2.2 Elasticiteit

## Scope

This chapter slice contains `2.2.1 Prijselasticiteit` and `2.2.2 Elasticiteit en omzet`.

Later paragraph folders for 2.2.3 and 2.2.4 are not accepted as proof and are not included in this chapter assembly.

## Student Route

The 2.2.1 route teaches students to:

- identify old and new values for price and quantity demanded;
- calculate percentage changes using the old value as denominator;
- calculate signed `Ev`;
- classify demand with `|Ev|`;
- end the answer with a plain-language economic interpretation.

The 2.2.2 route teaches students to:

- calculate `TO = P x Q` before and after a price change;
- compare old and new omzet;
- connect the omzet change to signed `Ev` and `|Ev|` classification;
- use the omzet rule for price increases and price decreases;
- give bounded pricing advice without profit or cost reasoning.
