# L1.7B-P23 Design Review

Date: 2026-05-28
Status: PASS WITH FLAGS

## Verdict

Design verdict: **PASS WITH FLAGS**.

The stop/handoff decision is accepted. L1.7B-P23 should close as a correct
stop/handoff sprint, not as a generated-output sprint.

Generating choice-only exit tickets for `1.1.2` and `1.1.3` would weaken the
exit-ticket specification. Those paragraphs require task forms that the current
exit-ticket engine and UI do not support.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is the `1.1.2` operation-chain analysis correct? | Yes. It correctly identifies old/new value selection, difference calculation, division by old value, percentage notation, index calculation, index-point versus percentage distinction, and explanation of the index-point trap. |
| Is the `1.1.3` operation-chain analysis correct? | Yes. It correctly identifies graph drawing from table data, economic axis convention, graph reading, interpolation, table-value selection, percentage-claim evaluation, and short explanation. |

| Does the sprint preserve the reviewed-output-only `Check` rule? | Yes. No source data or generated output exists for `1.1.2` or `1.1.3`, and `Check` remains blocked for those paragraphs. |

## Design Flags

- Current exit-ticket engine supports only choice tasks.
- `1.1.2` requires calculation/work input, final answer input,
  percent/index notation, and short explanation.
- `1.1.3` requires table/graph interaction, axis/point-placement or graph
  construction support, interpolation, and short explanation.
- No `Check` route may appear for `1.1.2` or `1.1.3` until reviewed generated
  checkpoint output exists.

## Boundary Confirmation

This design review does not authorize generated exit-ticket output for `1.1.2`
or `1.1.3`, target-exercise readiness, diagnostics, mastery decisions,
automatic sequencing, grading or summative use, student-facing AI, PV
projection, PV machine promotion, target-exercise promotion, CP-6 or Year 1
closure, Scale Gate 1, or broad companion scaling.
