## Original Specification

The game row must provide three aspect-specific practice engines plus one
shared route/skill-map layer.

The three games are:

- `Redeneren`;
- `Rekenen` / old skill-tree math practice;
- `Grafieken`.

`Stappenplan` is procedure support. It must not replace the primary math
skill-tree game.

Core-spec failures may not be carried as ordinary flags. If the primary math
route is still wrong, the review must return REVISE or PAUSE.
