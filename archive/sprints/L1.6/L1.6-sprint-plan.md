## Procedure/PV Contract Decision

Use a bounded A61 table-value route for internal procedure validation:

- student-facing name: `Tabelwaarden selecteren voor een berekening`
- PV template used internally: `select_table_values_trace`
- formal step IDs:
  - `read_question_target`
  - `check_table_headers_units`
  - `select_needed_values`
  - `label_selected_values`

The student page may teach the same ordinary learning route, but it must not
display `A61`, `PV`, `select_table_values_trace`, or any claim that PV is
student-facing. The route requires teacher-learning-quality review before
closure because Proof 002 was only bounded proof diversity.

## Decision Points

1. If the Part A flags are non-blocking for student-web, leave them as flags and
   record them. If they break companion parity or validation, fix Part A through
   the platform/approved content workflow before closure.
2. If graph/table skilltree units are still generator-blocked, either implement
   the missing platform generators or keep them out of student-facing skilltree
   paragraph mode. Do not expose blocked skills as runnable activities.
3. If A61 mapping cannot be validated without violating PV-G4 conditions, stop
   and close L1.6 as paused rather than weakening the boundary.

## Stop Conditions

- The plan does not list expected generated outputs and review artifacts.
- `1.1.3` cannot be reconciled with v5 without pretending the migrated target
  exercise is reviewed final.
- Any graph/table skill requires protected machine-reference edits outside an
  approved platform CLI/governance path.
- Any generated lesson output would need hand-patching in the lesson repo.
- Any A61/PV route is claimed classroom-ready without full student-facing build
  and teacher-learning-quality review.
- The adaptive seam becomes diagnostic, routing, mastery, sequencing, AI, or
  summative behavior.
- Mobile/dark-mode rendering has obvious overlap, clipping, unreadable graphs,
  or hidden controls.
