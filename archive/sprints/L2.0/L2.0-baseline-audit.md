## Scope

This audit covers the Book 1 Chapter 1.1 pilot surfaces after L1.7D:

- `1.1.1 Schaarste en economisch denken`
- `1.1.2 Percentages en indexcijfers`

sprint before L1.7B-R, GATE-L1.7B, and Scale Gate 1.
