# L2.0 Quality-Ref Status Language Standard

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: DRAFT FOR HUMAN REVIEW

## Purpose

Quality refs should make review state readable without overclaiming. This
standard documents how future records should use `PASS`, `PASS WITH FLAGS`,
`REVISE`, `FAIL`, and `FLAG`.

## Verdict Language

| Status | Meaning | Scale implication |
|---|---|---|
| `PASS` | Reviewed, no known material flags for the stated scope. | May support scale-gate evidence if current and generated from the approved workflow. |
| `PASS WITH FLAGS` | Reviewed, no open hard fails, usable for the stated controlled scope, but named flags remain. | Not automatically scale-ready. Flags must be classified and visible. |
| `REVISE` | Usable evidence is close but a product/review blocker remains. | Not closeable until fixed or explicitly waived by human decision. |
| `FAIL` | The artifact fails the review purpose. | Not usable as closure evidence. |
| `FLAG` | Legacy or Part A-style flag status. The record may be usable, but the flag must be interpreted in context. | Do not silently treat as `PASS WITH FLAGS`; either migrate through workflow or carry as legacy evidence. |

## Required Fields For Schema v2 Paragraphs

Future schema v2 quality refs should include:

- `partA.review.file`
- `partA.review.verdict`
- `partA.review.last_reviewed`
- `partA.review.flags` when flags remain
- `partA.review.fixed_flags` when flags were closed
- `companion.review_file`
- `companion.review_verdict`
- `companion.last_reviewed`
- `companion.hard_fails_open`
- `companion.student_facing_internal_codes`
- surface-by-surface status under `companion.surfaces`
- sprint-specific evidence blocks only when they are tied to real review
  records, such as `l16r_dual_coding`

## PASS WITH FLAGS Rule

`PASS WITH FLAGS` is allowed only when:

1. human or approved reviewer evidence exists for the stated scope;
2. there are zero open hard fails;
3. flags are named and routed;
4. no flag contradicts the artifact's core learning purpose;
5. the status does not imply broad scaling, final target-exercise review, or
   summative/product use.

Examples:

- A graph lesson whose main explanation has no graph is `REVISE` or `FAIL`,
  not `PASS WITH FLAGS`.
- A scaffolded graph MVP with visible values can be `PASS WITH FLAGS` if the
  sprint explicitly accepts scaffolded MVP status and routes harder variants
  forward.
- A procedure game that is not a full calculation engine can be `PASS WITH
  FLAGS` if the visible label says `Rekenen / stappenplan` and no product claim
  says full calculation coverage.

## Legacy Records

Legacy records such as `1.1.4-quality-ref.yaml` may remain in their current
shape during L2.0. They should not be hand-upgraded merely to satisfy a status
standard. A later targeted quality workflow may migrate consolidation records
to schema v2 if needed.

## Boundary Language

Quality refs and review records must not authorize:

- diagnostics;
- adaptive routing;
- mastery or sequencing decisions;
- grading or summative use;
- student-facing AI;
- PV projection;
- PV machine promotion;
- target-exercise promotion;
- CP-6 or Year 1 closure.
