## Verdict

was not closure-ready, but the second-revision review accepts the blocker fixes.

## Blocking Findings From Review

1. Answer placeholders revealed expected answers.
   - Numeric inputs used `challenge.expected_answer.value`, for example
     `Bijvoorbeeld 180`.
   - Percentage inputs used the expected percentage value.
2. Percentage-change dropdowns preselected old/new values.
   - This could silently select the wrong comparison for a task.
3. The task screen needed a visible pre-answer checklist.
   - The subtitle gave the general route, but the form needed local
     scaffolding.
4. Wrong-answer feedback was formative but too generic.
   - It did not distinguish wrong graph value, wrong old/new pair, or
     calculation error.
5. `1.1.2-quality-ref.yaml` did not yet include `grafiekenspel` under companion
   games.
