## Planned Questions First

Before an interview starts, show the reviewer this full question list. Then ask
one question at a time and record the answer.

### Student-Experience Questions

1. When you land on the `1.1.2` overview page, do you understand where you are
   and what you can do first?
2. In the `Oefenen` section, do the three routes `Redeneren`, `Rekenen`, and
   `Grafieken` make sense as different ways to practice?
3. Is it clear that `Grafiekenspel` is the route for reading graphs and using
   graph values?
4. When `Grafiekenspel` opens, do you know what to do before typing an answer?
5. Is the graph readable on a laptop screen?
6. Is the graph readable on a narrow phone-width screen?
7. Are the answer box, unit, and `Controleer` button clear enough?
8. After trying one correct and one incorrect answer, is the feedback useful?
9. Does the game feel like practice you can handle, or does it feel like a test
   before enough explanation?
10. What is the most confusing or frustrating part, if any?

Student-experience verdict options:

- PASS: ready for students; only minor improvements remain.
- REVISE: usable after concrete revisions.
- FAIL: major confusion risk or missing evidence that students can use it.

### Teacher-Learning-Quality Questions

1. Does the three-route `Oefenen` structure match the intended learning aspects:
   reasoning, calculation, and graphical representation?
2. Does `Grafiekenspel` align with the learning goals of `1.1.2 Percentages en
   indexcijfers`?
3. Is the first challenge sequence didactically sound: read the source, read
   the graph values, then calculate only when needed?
4. Is there enough prior knowledge and scaffolding for a 4 vwo student to start
   without the teacher repairing the task live?
5. Does the game connect graph reading to the paragraph's percentage/index
   learning, rather than becoming a separate mini-game?
6. Is the feedback useful as formative feedback, not only as right/wrong
   scoring?
7. Are weaker students supported enough and stronger students still given a
   meaningful practice route?
8. Is this safe to use as a real companion activity in `1.1.2` while L1.6 still
   needs to prove the same machinery on a fresh paragraph?
9. What must be fixed before closing L1.5G-E, if anything?

Teacher-learning-quality verdict options:

- PASS: classroom-ready; only minor improvements remain.
- REVISE: learning is likely after concrete revisions.
- FAIL: major didactic misalignment or insufficient evidence of learning.
