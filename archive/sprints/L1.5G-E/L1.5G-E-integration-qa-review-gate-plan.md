# L1.5G-E Integration, QA, And Review Gate Plan

Date: 2026-05-17
Status: technical QA planned; human review required before closure

## Purpose

L1.5G-E is the closure gate for the split L1.5G game track. It must prove that
the generated lesson output is reachable and usable after L1.5G-B, L1.5G-C,
and L1.5G-D, and it must prepare the formal student and teacher review gate
before L1.6 starts.

This is not a feature sprint. Do not add new game behavior unless a validation
or rendered-output defect makes a small fix unavoidable.

## Scope

Technical QA covers:

- graphical engine tests;
- graphical data validation tests;
- adaptive-seam regression tests;
- deploy/link/data checks through platform `deploy.js`;
- complete student-web validation for the graphical pilot paragraph `1.1.2`;
- complete student-web validation for `1.1.1` as the procedure-contract
  baseline paragraph;
- procedure-contract validation;
- Book 1 health check;
- platform regression tests;
- student-facing wording checks for internal codes and blocked-use claims;
- rendered screenshot QA for the `1.1.2` landing page and graphical game in
  desktop, mobile, light, and dark contexts where the browser can force or
  detect dark mode.

Human review covers:

- student-experience review of the three-aspect route and `grafiekenspel`;
- teacher-learning-quality review of the graphical MVP as a real paragraph
  activity inside `1.1.2`;
- lead-review consolidation.

## Acceptance Tests

Technical package acceptance:

- all listed commands pass, or failures are fixed or explicitly logged;
- screenshots are produced and inspected;
- generated output is produced through platform workflow only;
- no internal PV/unit code or blocked-use claim is visible in the checked
  student-facing surfaces;
- PV-G4 conditions remain preserved:
  - no PV machine promotion;
  - no student-facing PV projection;
  - no adaptive behavior;
  - no diagnostics;
  - no mastery, sequencing, AI, or summative use.

Gate closure acceptance:

- student-experience review returns PASS or a non-blocking PASS-equivalent
  decision accepted by the lead review;
- teacher-learning-quality review returns PASS or a non-blocking PASS-equivalent
  decision accepted by the lead review;
- lead review consolidates both specialist reviews and the technical QA;
- roadmap is updated with the exact final decision and evidence paths.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before closing L1.5G-E if:

- any validator, test, deploy, or Book 1 health check fails;
- rendered screenshots show clipping, unreadable graph labels, broken
  navigation, blank screens, or incoherent light/dark behavior;
- any student-facing surface leaks internal PV/unit codes or claims adaptive,
  diagnostic, mastery, sequencing, AI, or summative behavior;
- generated lesson output was hand-patched instead of regenerated;
- teacher or student review is missing;
- a human-review answer indicates that the graphical game or three-aspect
  routing is confusing enough to require revision.

## Expected Decision

If technical QA passes but human review is not yet recorded, the correct
decision is:

`READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW - NOT CLOSED`

L1.6 should not start until the formal review gate is closed or the roadmap is
explicitly updated to accept a different risk posture.
