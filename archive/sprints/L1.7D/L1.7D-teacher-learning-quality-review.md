## Verdict

Teacher-learning-quality verdict: **PASS WITH FLAGS**.

The landing-page information architecture is pedagogically better than the
previous equal-card design. It gives students a controlled preparation,
learning, practice, and extension route without making unsupported claims about
mastery, diagnostics, adaptive sequencing, grading, or summative use.
