## Decision Question

Are we ready to scale many companion paragraphs?

## Decision

No. The lesson side is **not ready for broad companion scaling**.

The project is ready for controlled foundation-hardening work only.

## Why

The current technical gates are green for the first three companion theory
paragraphs and Book 1:

- `1.1.1`, `1.1.2`, and `1.1.3` complete student-web validation pass.
- `1.1.1`, `1.1.2`, and `1.1.3` publisher-print validation pass.
- Procedure-contract validation passes with 341 checks.
- Book 1 health passes 26/26.
- Book 1 print scope passes 12/12.
- v5 target-exercise count validation passes with 54 records and 12/12/14/16.

But the qualitative evidence is not yet strong enough for broad scale:

- L1.6 initially missed a core dual-coding failure in a graph/table paragraph.
- L1.6R repaired that failure and added visual-value concordance, but the rule
  is not yet a reusable general gate.
- The graph game remains an MVP with scaffolded/value-labelled visuals.
- The three-aspect game row is routed but not quality-hardened.
- The shared skill-map engine contract does not exist yet.
- The exit-ticket/checkpoint surface is not yet part of a complete companion
  contract.
- Landing pages risk product sprawl as more surfaces are added.
- Target exercises are migrated or placeholders, not reviewed final.

## Allowed Next Work

Proceed with the foundation-hardening sequence:

1. `L1.7B Exit Ticket Game MVP + Companion Completion Contract`
2. `L1.7C-0 Shared Skill-Map Engine Contract`
3. `L1.7C Three-Aspect Game Quality Upgrade`

for those gates and uses the platform workflow with validators and review
records.
