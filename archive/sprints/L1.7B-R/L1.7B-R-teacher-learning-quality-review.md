## Required Carried Flag

`L1.7B-R-CF1 — Exit-ticket skill metadata mismatch.`

The `1.1.1` checkpoint is accepted for controlled pilot use because visible
tasks and labels align with `B01`/`B02`, but its internal `targetSkillIds` and
`skillScopeIds` still use `A43`/`A04`. These IDs may not be used for
diagnostics, adaptive routing, mastery, sequencing, coverage claims,
target-exercise promotion, CP-6 or Year-1 closure, PV projection/promotion,
Scale Gate 1 reliance, or broad scaling.

Before scaling exit tickets beyond the pilot, the platform must support
B-unit / paragraph-plan skill scoping or a reviewed mapping layer from
checkpoint data to paragraph skills.

## Boundary Confirmation

L1.7B-R does not authorize adaptive diagnostics, mastery decisions, automatic
sequencing, grading or summative use, student-facing AI, PV projection, PV
machine promotion, target-exercise promotion, CP-6 closure, Year 1 closure,
Scale Gate 1, or broad companion scaling.
