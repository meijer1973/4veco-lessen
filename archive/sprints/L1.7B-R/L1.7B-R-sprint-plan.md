## Product Contract For The MVP

The MVP must:

- use 3 to 5 short checkpoint tasks;
- use the shared skill-map engine in compact checkpoint mode;
- show student-facing skill labels only, never internal MTU IDs;
- give neutral feedback and self-check guidance;
- support retry/practice language;
- optionally suggest a practice surface such as Redeneren, Rekenen/stappenplan,
  or Grafieken as local practice support;
- keep all progress local and non-evidentiary.

The MVP must not:

- use `mastery`, `pass`, `fail`, `score`, `grade`, `summative`,
  `evidence`, `diagnose`, `adaptive`, `personalized route`, or equivalent
  student-facing product claims;
- read adaptive payload keys to change tasks, focus, order, difficulty, or
  route;
- store machine evidence for teacher or product decisions;
- activate `Check` for paragraphs without reviewed exit-ticket output;
- mutate protected references or target-exercise status;
- claim CP-6, Year 1, or Scale Gate 1 closure.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before implementation if:

- the work would import the untracked prototype directly;
- the product still needs mastery/pass/score/evidence/adaptive semantics to
  function;
- platform support must be implemented under `GAME-UX-2` but that lane is not
  active;
- GATE-EX5 or a human waiver is required before platform support may proceed;
- generated lesson output would need hand patches;
- internal MTU IDs would appear in student-facing copy;
- `Check` activation cannot be generator-owned and paragraph-scoped;
- any artifact would imply diagnostics, adaptive routing, mastery, sequencing,
  grading, summative use, student-facing AI, PV projection, or PV machine
  promotion.
