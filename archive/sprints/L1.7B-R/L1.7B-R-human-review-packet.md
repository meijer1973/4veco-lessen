## Student-Experience Questions

1. When opening the `1.1.1` landing page, is `Check` visible and understandable
   as a short checkpoint rather than a test or grade?
2. Does the exit-ticket page feel short enough for an end-of-paragraph
   checkpoint?
3. Do the four tasks clearly check scarcity, scarce means, opportunity costs,
   and free-is-not-free reasoning?
4. Is the feedback helpful and neutral, without suggesting pass/fail,
   mastery, diagnosis, grades, or summative assessment?
5. Do the practice links and route prompts help a student choose what to
   practise next without implying automatic sequencing?
6. Are internal IDs such as `A43`, `A04`, `B01`, or `B02` absent from visible
   student-facing text?
7. Does the page avoid the blocked product claims: adaptive diagnostics,
   mastery, sequencing, AI, summative use, PV projection, or PV promotion?
8. Do the desktop/mobile and light/dark screenshots show readable layout
   without overlap or horizontal scrolling?
9. What is still confusing, too test-like, overloaded, or under-explained?

Student-experience verdict options:

- PASS
- PASS WITH FLAGS
- REVISE
- FAIL

## Teacher-Learning-Quality Questions

1. Is the exit-ticket role pedagogically appropriate: a non-summative
   checkpoint with local practice feedback only?
2. Do the four tasks adequately sample the `1.1.1` concepts for an MVP:
   scarcity, scarce means, opportunity costs, and free-is-not-free reasoning?
3. Are the feedback and practice suggestions specific enough to support
   learning without becoming diagnosis or routing?
4. Does the generated checkpoint preserve ordinary student-facing language and
   avoid internal MTU/skill IDs in visible copy?
5. Does the internal metadata mismatch matter for closure: the checkpoint uses
   `A43`/`A04` as target/scope IDs, while the paragraph plan names `B01`/`B02`?
6. If that mismatch is accepted, what exact carried flag or platform follow-up

7. If that mismatch is not accepted, what must be revised before L1.7B-R can
   close?
8. Are remaining flags acceptable for controlled pilot use, or must any block
   closure before GATE-L1.7B?

Teacher-learning-quality verdict options:

- PASS

- FAIL
