# Chapter Plan: 2.1 Kosten en opbrengsten

## Scope

This chapter assembly combines the Part A printed textbook output for:

- `2.1.1 Kostenstructuren`
- `2.1.2 Opbrengsten, winst en break-even`
- `2.1.3 Marginale kosten en marginale opbrengsten`
- `2.1.4 Gemengde opgaven`

The first three paragraphs are theory paragraphs with `paragraaf`,
`opgaven`, and `antwoorden` files. The fourth paragraph is a consolidation
section with opgaven and antwoorden only.

## Asset Policy

Chapter-level assets are copied from the paragraph `_assets` folders into the
chapter `_assets` folder during `build_chapter.py`. The chapter markdown keeps
the same `_assets/<file>` references, so the chapter validator can compare the
aggregate assets with the paragraph sources.
