## Teaching and target coverage

One overview function supplies slides **1, 14 and 24**, including goals, route,
page/assignment, basis/independent/target and homework. Text and geometry agree;
only phase emphasis, marker and slide number change.

Slides 3–13 use **Houten puzzels**, an authored example explicitly marked
“Uitlegvoorbeeld — niet uit het boek”, not an assigned exercise. Functions:
`Pc = 26 − 0.20Q`, `Pp = 2 + 0.10Q`, `t = 6`; Q is puzzles per week.
Free equilibrium `(80,10)`; taxed quantity 60, Pc 14, Pp 8; burdens 4 and 2.
The method progresses through free equilibrium, supply in buyer prices, solving,
two prices at one quantity, graph/wedge and incidence. The final check rejects
old price plus whole tax by comparing Qv 50 with Qa 80. Prerequisite operations,
representation changes, assumptions and first practice are traced in the manifest.

Target context, data, original basis graph geometry and **all questions a–e**
appear on slides 15–17 without solutions. Answers follow on slides 18–23:

- a: `20 − 0.20Q = 2 + 0.10Q`; Q0 60 tassen/day, P0 €8/tas.
- b: `Pc = Pp + 3 = 5 + 0.10Q`; new Q 50 tassen/day.
- c: Pc €10 and Pp €7, both at Q 50; check €10 − €7 = €3.
- d: A+t through `(0,5)` and `(100,15)`; mark `(50,10)`, `(50,7)`
  and the vertical wedge. Original V/A and numeric scales remain intact.
- e: buyer pays €2 more; seller receives €1 less. Remitting €3 does not mean
  bearing €3; Pp is before production costs, not profit.

No missing or conflicting target source facts were found. Book 3 target records
