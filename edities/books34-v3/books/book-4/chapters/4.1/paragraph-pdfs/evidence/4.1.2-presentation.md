## Lesson and target coverage

The same overview source produces slides 1, 13 and 24, with matching text and
geometry except phase emphasis and slide number. Slides 2–12 teach/refresh
the required operations. Nachtkoepel and the small grower are explicitly
authored examples, with separate contexts and data; no assigned practice
question is used as the default worked example.

The inverse-price calculation needed for 14b is demonstrated on slide 9.
Slides 10–11 distinguish own-price movement from a change in another demand
factor. The teacher notes explain both effects together for 15c. Firm/market
quantity, uniform prices, domain limits and context/unit changes are explicit.

Target sources A/B and the complete editable figure appear on slides 14–15.
All five original subquestions appear without worked answers on slides 16–17.
Answers follow: 18a on slide 18, 18b on 19, 18c on 20–21, 18d on 22 and
18e on 23. P(60) = €18 per card; P(120) = €12. At €18, demand is 60 cards
per day. Capacity 240 does not make demand for 120 cards at €18 feasible.
